## 2026-11-21
- **The combo pauses on a Miss that doesn't end the pattern** (02:24 ET): it keeps its number, and the next Perfect, Good or Poor carries on from it. Thread: Systems & Code. Amends CoD-07 and, within Beat-State, Supplement 1's Miss State ("Chain resets to zero"). Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change).
- **O-52's test switch is a developer switch** (02:24 ET): built with the Basic Bot, to choose whether enemies act on the passage grid, the track grid or their own timers; easy to remove; left out of the release build. Thread: Systems & Code. IDs: O-52; OQ-054 (its O-52 half). Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: not built yet.
- **A completed pattern's combo carries over to the next pattern** (02:32 ET). Thread: Systems & Code. Amends CoD-07. Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change).
- **Developer switches live in one `dev_tools` module that can be removed** (02:32 ET), and it is left out of the release build. Thread: Systems & Code. Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: not built yet; `CLAUDE.md` holds the rule (this change).
- **What counts as a completed pattern** (11:29 ET; confirmed 12:09 ET). The author's words: "What counts as a completed pattern is option A", and "completed means being able to play through the full pattern (later patterns may be longer and/or more complex)". Option (a), as put to the author: a pattern played through to its end is complete, and a pattern that ends on Misses or the idle gap ends the combo; a pattern lasts 4 bars at Tier 1; the next pattern starts on the next bar, with no new opening attack; and its miss count starts fresh. He accepted these "as initial testing patterns". Thread: Systems & Code. Amends CoD-07. Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd`, `scripts/proto/beat_state_proto.gd` (this change).
- **How the next pattern is picked** (12:09 ET; the Tier 1 choice 12:16 ET). The author's words: "when a pattern completes, the next pattern depends on the same instances that started the initial pattern: this means the same pattern can be played back to back, or other patterns can be discovered as "Pattern Extensions" or "Pattern Switch-ups" so that the sound of combat is entirely composed by the player." For Tier 1 he chose the closing attack: the range tier of the completed pattern's last attack picks the next pattern, so the same tier repeats it and the other tier switches up to that tier's passage. Extensions need new passages (Tier 1.5). Thread: Systems & Code. Widens CoD-07. Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd`, `scripts/proto/beat_state_proto.gd` (this change).
- **Damage in Beat-State** (11:29 ET; clarified 12:09 ET). The author's words: "include taking enemy damage as a miss, so you can be hit once and still continue the pattern, twice resets. Taking environmental damage ends the pattern immediately." And, clarifying: "an enemy hit functions the same way as the player missed input, and instead of a missed note scratch, it should be more like a percussive amp-feedback buzz alongside the enemy hit-type". Built as: an enemy hit is a Miss for both Miss rules and for the combo pause; environmental damage ends the pattern, and the combo with it, at once. The buzz and the hit-type sound are wired with the Basic Bot, from Audio & SFX's design. Thread: Systems & Code. Amends CoD-07. Recorded in: the Systems & Code diary, 2026-11-21. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change); nothing calls the hooks until damage exists.

## Build calls by Systems & Code, confirmed by the author (2026-11-21)
- **A Miss doesn't use up the beat it was nearest to,** so a later press can still play that beat. Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (commit `d65309a`).
- **A Miss that is both the second in a row and the third in total is logged as `two_in_a_row`.** Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (commit `d65309a`).

## Build calls by Systems & Code (not rulings)
- **A press belongs to the pattern of the beat it's graded against;** a press that plays the next pattern's downbeat early completes the current pattern first. A damage hook belongs to the pattern running when it's called. The idle gap counts from the last press, across the bar line (2026-11-21). Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change).
- **A Switch-up's passage starts ahead, so its downbeat is heard on the bar line,** once no press can still belong to the old pattern; judged targets are keyed by bar and position, so each pattern keeps its own passage's targets (2026-11-21). Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change).
- **An enemy hit isn't a timed input:** it emits `enemy_hit_taken` instead of `judged`, plays no scratch, writes no growth-log entry and doesn't reset the idle gap (2026-11-21). Code: `scripts/rhythm/beat_state.gd` (this change).
- **Tests write their growth logs to `user://test_logs/` and delete that folder;** normal play keeps every file (2026-11-21). Code: `scripts/progression/growth_log.gd`, `tests/run_tests.gd` (this change).
