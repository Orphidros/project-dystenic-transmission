# Decision records: 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-21

Compiled by the Systems & Code thread on 2026-11-21 from the Project's records, on the author's instruction: every ruling the author makes, in any thread, gets a record in this folder (OQ-068, adopted and widened on 2026-11-20). The Project's canon documents stay the authority: GDD v4.16.0, Supplement 1 v1.8.0 and the Tier 1 Prototype Plan v2.2.0, and their later versions. Where this file and they differ, they win.

Each record gives the ruling, the thread where the author made it, its ID, and where the Project records it. "Code" lists the files and the commit that carry it, where code carries it. IDs: CoD- (rulings made in a Claude Code session), OQ- (the Open Questions Register), O- (dt-godot's open items), FLAG- (the GDD's flag register). The 2026-11-05 rulings are in `2026-11-05_design-gap-session-1.md`, where D-nn is CoD-nn.
