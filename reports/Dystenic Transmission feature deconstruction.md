# Rebuilding three Metroidvania lineages as original 3D design

Dystenic Transmission (DT) can take a great deal from REPLACED, Castlevania: Symphony of the Night (SotN) and MIO: Memories in Orbit, but only as principles. **None of the three is a 3D-character precedent for DT.** REPLACED is hand-drawn sprites, SotN is 2D, and MIO is a custom-engine painterly world. The real 3D-character precedents are Planet of Lana, Little Nightmares and Reanimal. What transfers from REPLACED is design intent: weight, lighting, camera staging and the rule that enemies stay on the left or right. From SotN it is the unlock loop (an ability opens the world, and the world rewards experimentation), plus a decompiled damage and growth model that DT can study and should not copy. From MIO it is traversal that bounds exploits with one shared energy pool, banked-or-lost currency, a loadout that includes the map, and a camera that spends depth on framing instead of depth-axis play. A deeper pass on narrative says DT's Wave-Lock chapters are a branch-and-bottleneck structure, where cost is controlled by gating revelations on "facts held" and keeping one fixed plot spine. A deeper pass on RPG layers says the cheapest depth comes from a budgeted loadout with unequal costs, growth credited only for graded rhythm hits, and benched members who earn a fraction of XP into their own pool. Free tooling is plentiful for plumbing (saves, quests, hitboxes, particles, materials) and absent for DT's defining parts: the rhythm evaluator, the plane-locked 3D side camera and ledge or grapple states are custom work. Everything below is an option for the author to weigh, not a ruling. Anything that would add a new Tier 1 (T1) system is marked as needing the author's confirmation.

The REPLACED correction matters most. The earlier survey treated REPLACED's "hundreds and hundreds" of hand-drawn animations as a cost benchmark for DT, but that cost belongs to a sprite pipeline. DT's skeletal route is the opposite choice, with reuse, retargeting and blending available, so the sensible cost worry is bespoke finishers and traversal clips, not raw clip count. The MIO closure matters for a different reason: the studio's best-documented design choices now come from reviews and interviews, with no postmortem to follow. The SotN correction is the one with direct design consequences, because fan-wiki rules for crits and drops are widely repeated and the code says otherwise. In the decompilation, a crit rate is the weapon's `criticalRate` plus `sqrt(LCK*2 + (rand & 15)) - 5`, compared against a random byte, and the crit effect comes from a "highest stat roll wins" mechanic ([src/dra/5D5BC.c](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/sotn-decomp/blob/master/src/dra/5D5BC.c)). There is no attack-versus-luck branch. Drops use a byte roll shifted by only about `(LCK + rand & 0x1F) / 20`, so even maximum luck moves the roll by roughly 5 out of 256, against the fan-wiki claim of "base rate x LCK / 100, capped at 50% and 25%" ([Castlevania Wiki: LCK](https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/LCK), search snippet only, page blocked). The code is the authority here, and the wiki is a likely simplification (inference). Hand-reading the code does carry one caveat: names such as `func_800FE044` are community guesses, so every rule below is the repo's reading of the binary.

## What each reference game teaches, rebuilt for 3D characters

The three games were deconstructed into four columns: how the feature works, how DT rebuilds it as an original with 3D characters, and the tier. Camera, motion and combat-feel research (notes 02 and 16) is merged into the REPLACED table because REPLACED's design intent is where those topics meet. Every row is a principle; no names, art, levels, music or code transfer.

### REPLACED and the cinematic 2.5D precedents

REPLACED launched 2026-04-14 on Windows and Xbox Series X|S after slipping from 2026-03-12, with Metacritic 76 on PC ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replaced_(video_game))). Its developers name lighting as the core of cinematic feel and built a custom Unity render pipeline for it ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=638436)). Its combat organising word is "weight", and the hero starts deliberately sluggish so that fluency is earned through story ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/04/14/replaced-combat/)). Since the pipeline cannot transfer, the table also draws on Planet of Lana (characters moved from 2D to 3D for "a more dynamic animation system", with painted assets, hidden shadow planes and a black foreground "stage") ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/exploring-the-art-and-animation-behind-hand-painted-odyssey-i-planet-of-lana-i-)), Little Nightmares, and Reanimal's varied chase angles ([VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/features/hands-on-reanimal-is-a-literal-new-dimension-in-horror-from-little-nightmares-studio-tarsier)).

Scope lessons from REPLACED are cautionary: it started in 2018, was announced at E3 2021, slipped repeatedly and eased late-game combat difficulty ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replaced_(video_game)); [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=638436)). DT's tier lock already guards against the first two. The third argues for assist options early, which MIO's boss erosion also supports (below).

### Castlevania: Symphony of the Night and its descendants

SotN dropped stage-by-stage progression for an open castle, inspired by Zelda and Super Metroid ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Symphony_of_the_Night)). Igarashi's stated approach for the successor was "how the game felt was more important than challenging new ideas", with the first design step being abilities that expand the map ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/a-i-bloodstained-i-q-a-with-koji-iga-igarashi)). The decompilation supplies the numbers. The study notes here describe rule shapes only; DT should not reuse any of SotN's tables, curves, shop lists or spell inputs.

| Feature | How it works | How DT rebuilds it originally | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ability-gated world | Transformations and items open already-seen areas ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Symphony_of_the_Night)) | DT's rule that a character only sees an interactable they can use is a stronger gate (hidden affordances, not blocked doors); a Vox-only and a Ciphera-only affordance in the T1 map tests it | T1 |
| Attack, defence and damage | Weapon ATT plus STR (half if ATT exceeds STR); per hit: weakness x2, resist x1/2, crit, then flat DEF subtraction with a floor of 1 and a 9999 cap ([src/dra/menu.c](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/sotn-decomp/blob/master/src/dra/menu.c); [5D5BC.c](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/sotn-decomp/blob/master/src/dra/5D5BC.c)) | Borrow the bounded extremes (floor and cap). **Avoid flat DEF subtraction**, which makes weak hits worthless and would undercut the Poor and Good rhythm tiers; use a proportional or percentage-floor rule (inference) | T1 |
| Crit flavour | Each base stat plus a random roll competes and the highest picks the crit's effect (double, armour-based, scaling, bonus) | Let each member's crit flavour follow their own identity; needs more than two characters to pay off | T2 |
| Growth and XP | Total-XP table; each level gives MP, hearts, a level-banded HP step and coin-flip attribute gains with a mercy rule; enemy XP falls by 2/3 per level the player exceeds the enemy, rises 25% per level above ([src/config_us.h](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/sotn-decomp/blob/master/src/config_us.h)) | DT's XP is spent, not automatic. Keep the diminishing-return idea per character pool and replace coin-flips with chosen spends that each have a legible marginal value (inference) | T2 |
| Equipment | Two hand slots plus head, armour, cape and two accessories; consumable weapons vanish unless a Duplicator is worn; one distinctive modifier per item (cost halving, enemy-DEF shred) | One memorable modifier per gear item gives identity without new systems. **Avoid items that delete a core cost** (stamina, ammo) unless a large sink offsets them | T1.5 |
| Familiars and use-based growth | Familiar XP per kill is about the bit length of enemy XP divided by familiar level, so gains shrink as it levels | Use-based ability growth shaped like log2(challenge / rank), credited for graded hits only | T2 |
| Spells by input | Eight fixed directional inputs, MP 5 to 50, longer inputs cost more | Price rhythm-input specials by sequence length and accuracy tier | T2 |
| Shop gated by progress | Librarian stock unlocks by a castle-progress counter ([src/st/lib/e_shop.c](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/sotn-decomp/blob/master/src/st/lib/e_shop.c)) | A vendor whose stock unlocks at recovered signal fragments or story beats; Hollow Knight's Salubra unlocks stock at charm counts, per search summary | T1.5 |
| Portrait of Ruin switching | Free switching between two characters, a joint "Dual Crush", paired puzzles; MP drains while the partner fights ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Portrait_of_Ruin); partner AI details are secondhand) | A shared-cost switch layer for two to five members; keep the idle member visibly doing something | T2 |
| Tactical Soul, glyphs, shards | Absorb-to-learn kits with equip limits and MP cost ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Aria_of_Sorrow)) | Enemy-affinity-tiered learning; see the RPG section | T2 |
| Inverted castle | Content-reuse multiplier via a flip | DT's Decay Map reuses space as it changes over time, a more original multiplier | T2 |
| Boss fairness | "The developer who creates the boss must beat their own boss without taking a hit and only using a dagger" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/a-i-bloodstained-i-q-a-with-koji-iga-igarashi)) | Each boss and each rhythm tier must be clearable with the starting character and no stat edge | T1.5 |

Two cautions come from the speedrun record. Level-ups, relics and vessels trigger screen pauses, and wolf-form clipping lets players bypass a story gate ([TASVideos](https://tasvideos.org/GameResources/PSX/SymphonyOfTheNight)). For DT this means no single-ability gates that clipping can break, and deliberate 3D streaming volumes instead of screen-edge room loads. Bloodstained's Kickstarter stretch goals (co-op, boss rush, roguelike mode, many ports) coincided with two years of slippage ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloodstained:_Ritual_of_the_Night)), a direct argument for the scope lock. Gaps remain: the decompiled enemy tables, two-handed rules and map-completion figures were not read, and ZKeene's FAQ returned 403, so none of the decompilation readings were cross-checked against the community's own.

### MIO: Memories in Orbit and 3D-rendered side views

The engine is described only as the studio's own, and the camera is "quite far away for gameplay reasons" ([GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/most-challenging-was-letting-go-of-ideas-we-liked-interview-with/z2783)). Metacritic runs 81 to 83, with the weaknesses being long platforming runs that restart "from the very first step", brutal bosses and costly Nacre loss ([AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/mio-memories-in-orbit-review); [But Why Tho](https://butwhytho.net/2026/01/mio-memories-in-orbit-review/)).

## Branching narrative: a bottleneck structure with facts-held gates and a single status enum

The deeper narrative pass converges on one structural reading of DT. Ashwell's taxonomy names Branch and Bottleneck as the pattern that "has to be used in a fairly large piece" and delivers "a somewhat-distinctive story", while Sorting Hat means the author "may end up effectively having to write several different games" ([IFWiki mirror](https://www.ifwiki.org/Standard_Patterns_in_Choice-Based_Games)). DT's Wave-Lock chapters fit Branch and Bottleneck with a Sorting Hat flavour, so the cost warning applies: state must accumulate across enough content to pay off, and variants multiply writing (inference). Shipped games contain the cost the same way. Until Dawn kept "the fundamental story" fixed and surfaced consequences through its Butterfly Effect, yet its co-writer said holding every branch in mind was "very difficult" ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2015/10/18/afterwords-until-dawn.aspx); [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/until-dawn-writer-the-butterly-effect-ridiculously-exciting/)). The Quarry's writer called tracking where nine characters were the hardest challenge ([SegmentNext](https://segmentnext.com/the-quarry-interview)). Tracking, not prose, is the burden, and the standard mitigation is a per-member status enum.

**Ordering and abandonment.** For the Information Ordering Contract, the cheapest mechanism that holds up against the sources is to tag each revelation with a prerequisite set of facts the player must already hold and let the Wave-Lock gate only on those tags, with unmet prerequisites defaulting to the ambiguous Distorted or Fragmented signal states rather than a hard block. This borrows Failbetter's quality-gated storylets ([Failbetter](https://www.failbettergames.com/echo-bazaar-narrative-structures-part-two)) and echoes The Witcher 3's Bloody Baron, where a reveal was scheduled after the player learned he sheltered Ciri ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bloody_Baron)). For FLAG-13, a single enum per member (present, injured, abandoned, deceased, returned) keeps writing near linear in cast size, because abandoned and deceased share most absence variants and differ in a few lines and in how the instrument falls silent (inference). Mass Effect 2's suicide mission is the best model of survival computed from stored variables at one climax, though its exact formula lives in an image flowchart that could not be read ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicide_Mission_(Mass_Effect_2))). Octopath's reviewers warn about the opposite failure, eight members who rarely interact ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/134209/octopath-traveler-2-review-score)), which argues for one cross-character beat per Wave.

**Loss and interiority.** The Witcher 3's lead quest designer killed a character because "I needed an actual event that would break her inside", and said it worked because the character was well built up beforehand ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/the-witcher-3-developer-on-hard-decisions-killing-important-characters/)). The rule for DT is that loss quality depends on pre-loss screen time, so each member needs early scenes before any absence. Darkest Dungeon's stress shape (threshold, dramatised check, per-state barks) matches DT's hidden stress value, but the displayed numbers and splash must become animation and audio tells, and the bark grid should shrink to two characters by two or three tells for T1 ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-i-darkest-dungeon-s-i-affliction-system)). Disco Elysium's skills-as-voices suggest each vocal mode could offer or withhold lines by investment, at a cost of voices times scenes, so only a few scenes per Wave should carry interjections ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_Elysium)). Frostpunk's two coupled meters suggest two band-level values rather than five per-member numbers, with Slant's critique as the warning that meters need visible operational consequences ([Slant](https://www.slantmagazine.com/games/review/frostpunk)).

**Music as consequence.** Undertale carries morality through tempo, pitch and tonality drift of existing leitmotifs, which is far cheaper than composing branch tracks ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/an-examination-of-leitmotifs-and-their-use-to-shape-narrative-in-undertale-part-2-of-2), track-level claims unverified). One theme per member with parametric degradation tied to tone counters would fit DT's audio stack (inference). UNBEATABLE, a band fighting police in a world where music is outlawed, is the closest premise match, and its writer's stance, "Once you say too much, you risk robbing someone of their own experience", supports DT's no-label choice rule ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/narrative-notebook-5-unbeatable-s-music-video-vibes-can-t-be-beat)). Until Dawn is the control case for the opposite approach, a visible icon that told players when to save-scum. Signalis rejects good, bad or true labels and ties endings to overall playstyle ([PlayStation Universe](https://www.psu.com/news/signalis-doesnt-have-a-good-bad-or-true-ending-says-developer/)), consistent with DT's rule.

**Ending shapes.** The sources support four options with different trade-offs, none a ruling. One is a roster vector plus one tone verdict, with endings assembled from modular epilogue cards per member, which scales linearly with cast. Two is a NieR shape, three or four authored endings keyed to a few variables plus cheap minor endings (26 in total, five of them main ([GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/guides/71797-nier-automata-a-guide-to-all-26-endings-no-spoilers))), which is cheapest for a solo developer and can host FLAG-09's chord-gated secret ending. Three is perspective replay, retelling the spine from another member with new information, which ties FLAG-09 to New Game+ (FLAG-12). Four is a storylet ending, where scenes gated by qualities play in a published priority order. Witcher 2's developers later clarified "16 endings" as 16 "states of the world" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Witcher_2:_Assassins_of_Kings)), which suggests counting world states rather than authoring finales. My synthesis, with no published source, is that ending conditions should read only flags that already exist for gameplay reasons, and that every ending-feeding flag should have a scene or audio consequence elsewhere. That rule also answers Telltale's critique that decisions have "only a superficial impact" ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/im-over-the-fact-that-choices-dont-really-matter-in-telltale-games)).

**Implementation in Godot.** The implementation note converges on one `NarrativeState` autoload holding bounded-integer qualities and a flag set, with a per-member status enum and the ending matrix as a data table. Dendry's qualities with `view-if` and `choose-if` expressions are the small open model ([aucchen/dendry](https://github.com/aucchen/dendry), MIT); Questify's pattern of routing conditions through a query signal you answer yourself keeps your own store as the source of truth ([Questify](https://github.com/TheWalruzz/godot-questify), MIT); Ren'Py separates persistent profile data from per-save state and supports custom merges such as a union of endings seen ([Ren'Py docs](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/persistent.html)). Dialogue Manager reads and mutates state through GDScript, with `extra_game_states` and `locals` scoping ([docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nathanhoad/godot_dialogue_manager/main/docs/Conditions_Mutations.md)). Because its conditions are arbitrary GDScript, a static checker cannot analyse them, so conditions should be limited to reads of named qualities. Validation should start with a headless seeded random-playthrough bot (Dendry ships `random-test`), add a deterministic breadth-first explorer over flag and quality snapshots at T1.5 because random bots miss endings that need strategic play ([Choice of Games forum](https://forum.choiceofgames.com/t/one-hopefully-quick-question-re-randomtest/42027), snippet), and add graph checks later. Saves should be JSON with a `schema_version` and migration functions and should never decode with `allow_objects` ([Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_fileaccess.html)). Study The Intercept for structure, since it is a small complete branching script under MIT ([inkle/the-intercept](https://github.com/inkle/the-intercept)).

**T1 additions: three approved by the author on 2026-11-05, one still open.** The author approved the pair-bond integer, the Ciphera rig assist and the growth-credit log for Tier 1 on 2026-11-05. The post-Wave "echo" stays an open option. This ruling isn't yet recorded in the GDD or the repo's CLAUDE.md scope lock. The original framing follows. The party layer note proposes a single hidden Vox and Ciphera pair-bond integer, written by rhythm performance and the micro-choice and logged but unseen, as a stub for later cohesion effects (Persona and Fire Emblem principle; [Megami Tensei Wiki](https://megatenwiki.com/wiki/Confidant), search summary). The same note proposes a real-time Ciphera rig assist outside the active-member rule, after Child of Light's Igniculus ([VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/guides/child-of-light-s-battle-system-an-introduction-and-tips-and-tricks)). The narrative notes offer a post-Wave "echo" playback of which tones were struck. And a growth-credit log (graded hits recorded but not yet spent) is the T1 half of use-based growth. None of these is in the locked T1 list, so each is an option, not a recommendation.

## RPG layers: cheap depth comes from budgets, graded growth and catch-up pools

The first-pass and gap-fill RPG notes agree on a small set of ideas, merged here. Three build axes recur. A fixed-budget loadout with unequal item costs is cheapest per authored item: Hollow Knight starts with 3 charm notches and reaches 11 ([Hollow Knight wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Charm_Notches)), Nine Sols' Jades cost 1 to 3 Computing Power ([GINX](https://ginx.tv/en/nine-sols/jade-slots)), and Lost Crown's amulets cost 1 to 3 points ([Guides4Gamers](https://guides4gamers.com/prince-of-persia-the-lost-crown/poi/amulets/)). Content scales as items times cost tiers times budget growth while authoring scales only with items, and each budget increase doubles as an exploration reward (inference). A mutually exclusive branch choice is the second axis, and Sundered's Elder Shards, which can be corrupted into an ability or burned for currency with three endings keyed to shard use, are the clearest example ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundered)). Absorb-to-learn kits are the third (Bloodstained shards, Grime), and Salt and Sacrifice's mage-to-gear-family mapping is the working model of "kill a specific enemy class to unlock a gear family" ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/?p=317471), search summary). Blasphemous 2's paired-slot resonance, which activates when two same-category pieces sit in adjacent slots, shows how a handful of items can create combinatorial depth ([Funario guide](https://funario.pages.dev/posts/every-altarpiece-resonance-in-blasphemous-2), a low-grade guide site), and DT could pair across Vox and Ciphera so the switch layer matters to builds (inference, option only).

**Three shapes, one hybrid.** Option A is a notch-budget loadout plus use-based tool growth, cheap to author and balance for two characters but at risk of shallow stat choices. Option B is absorb-and-learn, the strongest tie to the "Knowledge is the weapon" pillar and FLAG-39, but it needs authored content per enemy type. Option C is corrupt-or-burn exclusive branches signalled by tones, which compounds with the narrative but carries build-lock risk and a hard-to-balance power gap between ending paths. A hybrid fits the respec evidence: free swaps for loadout and gear, a costed or limited respec for XP-bought stats, and no refund for growth earned by play. Valfaris's irreversible Blood Metal spend draws complaints ([GameCritics](https://gamecritics.com/jarrod-johnston/valfaris-review/), search summary), and players complain that high respec costs in Path of Exile 2 "punish" experimentation ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/path-of-exile-2-is-dropping-loot-more-generously-but-early-access-players-are-still-being-bankrupt-by-the-action-rpgs-high-respec-costs-why-am-i-being-punished-for-trying-out-things), search summary). No developer postmortem on respec, stat bloat or dead stats was found for any Metroidvania, so those judgements rest on player evidence.

**Growth without grinding.** Use-based growth is exploited wherever the system cannot tell harmless from meaningful action: Final Fantasy II players attack allies, and Morrowind and Oblivion skills can be trained on harmless targets ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_II); [UESP](https://app.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Cheats)). The fix toolkit for DT, all inference, is to credit growth only for graded outcomes (Perfect and Good on a live threatening enemy count, Poor and Miss do not), cap gains per encounter, apply diminishing returns per rank (Dead Cells and Kenshi show the shape), scale by the enemy's affinity tier against the character's tier, and never credit friendly or self-inflicted damage, which DT can detect because it knows the damage source. Kawazu's point about Romancing SaGa, that frustration is managed by letting players find ways to raise their odds ([Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/saga-series-creator-says-giving-players-gameplay-driven-highs-matters-more-than-a-well-written-story/), search summary), suggests showing what raises the chance that a technique "sparks". Rhythm games tie timing to multipliers instead of growth: Metal: Hellsinger's Fury multiplier and NecroDancer's coin multiplier both reset on a miss ([GameCritics](https://gamecritics.com/?p=47231)), and NecroDancer added auto-calibration for latency. Only Grime gates growth on timing, via parry-to-absorb ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/gaming/grime-enemy-skills-traits-hunt-points-guide)). Because a timing-gated growth system punishes latency, calibration is part of the system, not an extra.

**Death, research and catch-up.** Death-penalty families split into recover-from-death-spot (Salt and Sanctuary) and bank-or-lose (Dead Cells cells, MIO Nacre). Blasphemous adds a persistent guilt debuff tied to a recoverable fragment, cleared by any boss kill ([Blasphemous wiki](https://blasphemous.wiki.gg/wiki/Guilt)). DT already has blackout, injury accumulation and escort, so a Blasphemous-style debt would reuse existing systems, with unspent XP as the exposed asset (inference). For research-before-craft no Metroidvania precedent was found. The nearest are Subnautica-style scan-N-fragments (duplicates worthless) and Satisfactory's MAM, where one trigger item opens a small typed tree ([Satisfactory wiki](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/MAM)); both fit FLAG-39's engine-parts arc at T2. Per-character XP has one developer-side source: a Game Developer article names reserve members falling behind as a problem and the Pokemon XP Sharer as a standard fix ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/make-grinding-fun---the-synergy-of-the-battle-system-and-the-progression-system-in-jrpgs-turn-based-strategy-games)). DT's spent-XP model suits a split: a fraction of the active character's income flows into each benched member's own pool, while spending stays per character. Bloodstained's active familiar earning XP from the player's kills is a mild precedent ([Fandom](https://bloodstained.fandom.com/wiki/Shard_Stand)). Because switching is core and a locked-out member blocks character-only interactables, falling behind hurts DT more than a typical party game (inference).

**Party, bonds, dispatch and hub.** Trine's three heroes are the model for "each member owns a verb the world is gated on", and Trine 4 loosened combat roles so players need not swap to the Knight for fights ([WCCFtech](https://wccftech.com/trine-4-hands-on-interview-e3-2019/)). The lesson is to keep combat flexible and put hard gates in traversal, with at least one swap needed per room. For combos, Sea of Stars uses up to 3 points built by timed play ([Gamepur](https://vip-develop.gamepur.com/guides/sea-of-stars-combat-basics-combo-points-live-mana-lock-breaking-timed-hits)), and Chrono Trigger unlocks dual techs when each member knows prerequisite singles and has fought together, with 45 dual and 15 triple techs authored ([Chrono Trigger Wiki](https://chronotrigger.wiki.gg/wiki/Techs)). Both are T2 and out of T1 scope. Chrono also left Magus without combos to save content, which suggests Ciphera as a "chord conductor" who enables pairs instead of authoring a finisher per partner (inference). For dispatch, Dragon Age: Inquisition's war table shows event-style missions with collect-report gating ([Fextralife](https://dragonage3.wiki.fextralife.com/War+Table)), and real-time timers invite clock exploits, so event-based or room-count timers are preferable (inference). Fire Emblem's own designers reset after losing units ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/even-the-people-behind-fire-emblem-hit-reset-when-somet-465971876)), so DT's optional permadeath will draw reloads and should pair with autosave-on-injury at T2 or later. Octopath's per-character path actions are the closest sourced analogue to "see only what your skills reveal", with the invasive half carrying a reputation cost ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopath_Traveler)), and T1 can test it with one NPC who reads differently to each character. The Lost Vikings, where one death makes the level unwinnable ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_Vikings)), is the failure DT's blackout-and-drag escort should avoid.

## A phased plan: Borrow, Adapt, Enhance, Avoid across tiers

The plan consolidates every note. All items are options for the author. Principles are rebuilt in DT's own terms, and no names, art, levels, music or code are copied. The T1 table follows the Tier 1 prototype phases (1 foundation, 2 player controller, 3 combat, 4 melee enemy, 5 rhythm evaluator, 6 flying enemy and Heavy Unit, 7 integration, 8 tuning) from the earlier survey's mapping.

### Tier 1: the locked prototype

T1 is Vox and Ciphera, 3 to 5 rooms, Basic Bot, Flying Drone and Heavy Unit, basic rhythm combat, basic switching, Wave-Lock and one micro-choice. Permadeath, combos and aerial switching stay out.

## Conclusion

The most useful result is a split in what these reference games can give DT. Their systems transfer as principles, but their pipelines do not: REPLACED's sprite craft, MIO's custom engine and SotN's 2D rooms are all different problems from a plane-locked skeletal character in real 3D. What does transfer is the structure of constraints. Bounded damage extremes, a single traversal energy pool, graded-only growth credit, one fixed spine with variants gated on facts held, and a status enum per member are all cheap, testable and original once rebuilt. The fact that no open-source Godot project solves the plane-locked 3D side camera or rhythm judgement means the DT-defining work is also the work that cannot be borrowed, so the first verifiable increment should be a running room with the camera rig and a rhythm-graded hit, as the project's own rule demands.

A second implication is about evidence. The decompilation showed that two widely repeated fan rules were wrong, and the MIO and REPLACED corrections show how quickly secondhand dev facts drift. DT's design choices should lean on the code and primary pages and treat every unverified item below as a to-do, not a premise.
