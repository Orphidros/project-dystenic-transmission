# Primary evidence via Steam news API: mechanics claims (round 2, track 07)

Research date: 2026-11-05. Follows `05_primary_mechanics.md`. Scope: `00_round2_scope.md`.

## Which mechanics claims can developer posts settle?

### Takeaway
The Steam news API worked (the HTML pages did not), but developer patch notes rarely state rule numbers. Only **one** claim was upgraded to [P1] with a settling quote: Sea of Stars combo points persist after battle (Dawn of the Equinox). A second claim got partial [P1] support: Lost Crown's amulet-holder count (3 at start, up to 9 more) and slot costs (1 and 3 for named amulets). Every other claim, including all the Darkest Dungeon stress claims, remains [S] with "no primary source found" in the feeds I read. The feeds are capped at 100 items and only reach back to each game's early news, so absence from the feed is weak evidence, not contradiction.

### Method and app ID confirmation
- Route: `https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=<ID>&count=100&maxlength=0`, plus a regex search of the full post bodies. Opened and searched in full (item counts in brackets).
- IDs were confirmed first with `store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails` (name and developer returned): Hollow Knight 367520, Team Cherry [100 items]; Darkest Dungeon 262060, Red Hook Studios [100]; Dead Cells 588650, Motion Twin [100]; Sea of Stars 1244090, Sabotage Studio [64]; Nine Sols 1809540, RedCandleGames [64]; Prince of Persia The Lost Crown 2751000, Ubisoft Montpellier [33]; Salt and Sanctuary 283640, Ska Studios [35]; Blasphemous 2 2114740, The Game Kitchen [48]. MIO: my guessed ID 1825360 resolved to nothing; a store search gave 1672810 "MIO: Memories in Orbit" [feed searched, items not counted]. I did not read its developer field, so treat that ID as name-matched only.
- Post authors are the studios' Steam community accounts (all feed label "Community Announcements"), so they count as developer-authored [P1]. The few press reposts in the feeds (PCGamesN, RPS) are not used as evidence.
- Not done: developer sites, official manuals, and in-game data. No decompilation. Budget stopped at about 12 calls.

Note on links: post links use the `store.steampowered.com/news/app/<ID>/view/<gid>` form built from the API `gid`; the API itself returned `steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/<gid>` URLs. I did not open the store-form links, so the gid is the reliable locator.

### Cited Findings (other)
- [P1] Hollow Knight patch notes confirm Salubra's Blessing as a distinct charm effect and overcharm as a tracked state ([Team Cherry posts](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/367520)).
- [P1] Blasphemous 2 patch notes show the altarpiece capacity is an upgradeable stat with a price ("the cost of upgrading your Altarpiece capacity", [post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2114740/view/1784506359177799)) and that resonances are categorised ("Adjusted some of the resonances so that their categories match up more appropriately", same post). Numbers are not given.

### Inferences
- Patch notes record changes, not rules. Rule numbers (notch prices, stress thresholds, loss percentages) will mostly need the game data or manuals. For DT these are mechanics-to-borrow, not facts to cite, so [S] numbers should be treated as inspiration only.
- Sea of Stars changed combo persistence from reset to persist after launch, which suggests the original reset design was a pain point (my reading; the post only says it adds options for opening encounters).

### Gaps
- Darkest Dungeon (first game) stress rules, Dead Cells death penalty, Nine Sols computing budget, Hollow Knight notch counts and Salubra prices, Salt and Sanctuary percentages, MIO death rule, Blasphemous 2 resonance total, Sea of Stars combo cap: no primary source found in Steam feeds. Next routes: Red Hook's own forum archive, Motion Twin's devblogs, Team Cherry's wiki-free sources, and in-game data or manuals.
- The MIO app ID was name-matched only.

## Design questions this raises for DT
1. **Combo persistence (T2).** Sea of Stars now keeps points after battle [P1]. If combos are ever approved, should DT's points persist between Waves or reset? Persistence reads as a puzzle-opening tool in that game, not only a damage tool.
2. **Loadout budget (T1 equipment).** Lost Crown starts with 3 holders and grows to 12 with 1-3 slot costs [P1 partial]. Should DT's budget start at 3 for two characters, and does the growth come from exploration finds?
3. **Stress or blackout ceiling.** No primary source settles Darkest Dungeon's 200 outcome. Which of Death's Door-style injury or a reset should DT's blackout use? The author decides without a reference.
4. **Death penalty.** The primary record has only salt being stolen and returned by killing a thief (Salt and Sanctuary). Should DT's unspent XP be recoverable from a dropped location or banked? No developer number exists to borrow.
5. **Thresholds for the pair-bond integer.** No primary source covers bond thresholds. Should DT define them from its own rhythm data first?
